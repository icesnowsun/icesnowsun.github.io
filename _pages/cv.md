### Research interests

Methods for learning from imperfect clinical and population health data:
longitudinal disease trajectories, cohort representativeness and external
validity, and the evaluation of statistical and predictive models before they
inform care. Applications to date in chronic kidney disease, sickle cell
disease, and national COVID-19 research cohorts.

### Research and professional experience

**Stanford University, School of Medicine** — Stanford, CA
*Biostatistician II, Division of Nephrology* · Apr 2022 – present

- **Comparing statistical models for identifying rapid kidney function decline**
  in a CKDu cohort in Sri Lanka (n = 292, followed since 2017). Proposed and
  implemented the linear mixed-effects and latent class mixed-effects
  comparisons alongside per-individual OLS slopes, fitted both pooled and
  sex-stratified. First-author poster, JSM 2024. The study provided the analytic
  basis for a subsequent machine-learning study of CKDu progression in the same
  cohort (WCN 2026 abstract; manuscript in preparation, second author).
- **Representativeness and generalizability of a national research cohort.**
  Applied a published similarity-based generalizability framework to compare
  RADx-UP participants against 2020 U.S. Census benchmarks across geographic and
  intersectional demographic strata, showing that aggregate scores conceal
  subgroup underrepresentation. Designed the radar-chart and map-based
  presentation adopted by the study team, and derived an alternative
  power-weighted aggregation. First-author poster, JSM 2025; manuscript in
  preparation.

**University of Mississippi Medical Center** — Jackson, MS
*Biostatistician; promoted to Biostatistician II in 2021* · Jul 2019 – Feb 2022
Department of Data Science, Bower School of Population Health

- First-author study of QTc duration in sickle cell disease (293 adults, 121
  children), using multivariable regression and a published Jackson Heart Study
  model; contributed with J. F. Maher to a sensitivity analysis excluding adult
  diuretic users.
- Analyzed metabolic changes during sickle cell pain crises; coauthored an
  amino-acid study using partial least squares-discriminant analysis and genetic
  research on QT prolongation.
