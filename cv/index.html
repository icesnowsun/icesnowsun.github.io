Research interests

Statistical and computational methods for kidney and population health; longitudinal modeling; model evaluation and population representativeness; reproducible analysis; responsible evaluation of AI in healthcare.

Experience

Stanford University, School of Medicine — Stanford, CA Biostatistician II, Division of Nephrology · Apr 2022 – present

  • Compared OLS, linear mixed-effects, and latent class mixed-effects models in R to identify rapid kidney function decline in 292 Sri Lankan participants in the Kidney Progression Project. Conducted pooled and sex-stratified analyses and wrote the first-author JSM 2024 poster; planned and validated analyses and interpreted classification differences and censoring limitations with a faculty mentor.
  • Computed demographic representativeness scores and programmed comparisons of RADx-UP against 2020 U.S. Census data to surface demographic gaps obscured by aggregate summaries. Wrote the first-author JSM 2025 poster.
  • Developed reusable RADx-UP trial data workflows and R/SAS functions for baseline tables, standardized differences, and missing-data summaries.
  • Designed and launched a web-based flowchart tool for fellows without coding experience, supporting multiple export formats and recording over 200 uses from June 2025 to January 2026.

University of Mississippi Medical Center — Jackson, MS Biostatistician; promoted to Biostatistician II in 2022 · Jun 2019 – Feb 2022 Department of Data Science, Bower School of Population Health

  • First author of a QTc study in 293 adults and 121 children with sickle cell disease, using multivariable regression and a published Jackson Heart Study model; contributed a sensitivity analysis excluding adult diuretic users.
  • Analyzed metabolic changes during sickle cell pain crises; coauthored an amino-acid study using partial least squares-discriminant analysis and genetic research on QT prolongation.

Georgetown University — Washington, DC Research Assistant, Department of Biostatistics · Jan – Dec 2018

  • Prepared data, programmed statistical analyses, and contributed to analysis plans for clinical and laboratory studies.
