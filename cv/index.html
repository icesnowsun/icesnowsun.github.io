Professional Summary

Biostatistician working with clinical and population health teams in nephrology. Interests span longitudinal modeling of kidney function, model evaluation and sample representativeness, reproducible analysis, and responsible evaluation of AI in healthcare.

Experience

  • 2022 - present

    Stanford, CA

    Biostatistician II, Division of Nephrology
    Stanford University, School of Medicine
    • Compared OLS, linear mixed-effects, and latent class mixed-effects models in R to identify rapid kidney function decline in 292 Sri Lankan participants; conducted pooled and sex-stratified analyses and wrote the first-author JSM 2024 poster, planning and validating analyses and interpreting classification differences and censoring limitations with a faculty mentor.
    • Computed demographic representativeness scores and programmed comparisons of RADx-UP with 2020 U.S. Census data to identify demographic gaps obscured by aggregate summaries; wrote the first-author JSM 2025 poster.
